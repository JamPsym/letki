Power and return evidence
A DC F/In2 resistor mesh at 0.1 mm pitch estimates VCC west/east 16.177 mOhm
and north/south 4.762 mOhm, or 40.44/11.90 mV at an illustrative 2.5 A. The
0.2 mm result was 17.474/5.069 mOhm and missed eight barrels; the finer mesh
missed none. Copper is modelled as 35/17.5 um, resistivity 1.724e-8 ohm*m,
with assumed 25 um barrel plating. B copper, contact/solder/wire resistance,
temperature, array current distribution and AC/EMI are excluded. This is an
estimate, not a measured limit, error bound or thermal rating.
The reviewed In1 slots end inside the board, away from M2/edge-to-edge cuts.
A 0.2 mm GND sheet comparison gives W/E3.003 and N/S2.944 mOhm versus
2.873/2.817 mOhm before the slots, about 4.5% greater. Connectivity, erosion
geometry and DC estimates do not qualify high-frequency return impedance.
