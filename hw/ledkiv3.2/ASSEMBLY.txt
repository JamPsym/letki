Fitting order
1. Identify the board and parts before soldering. Keep power disconnected.
   U1 must be CH32V006E8R6/QSOP24,3.9x8.7mm,pitch0.635mm. The owned
   CH32V006F8P6/TSSOP20 does not fit. CH32V007E8R6 is not an approved
   assembly substitute. Electronics now records50V006E8R6/QSOP24 ordered by
   the user from AliExpress; physical receipt and lot checks remain pending.
2. Fit rear resistors/capacitors, U1/U2 and MOSFETs using the pad numbers and
   pin1/body marks. U2 is SN74HC138PWR,inverting; do not use HCT238.
   Q1-Q8 are P-channel AO3415A; Q9-Q16 are N-channel DMN2056U-7.
   These share SOT23 G1/S2/D3 but have opposite channel types.
   The ordered AO3415/AO3414 candidates require a reviewed assembly overlay
   and lot qualification; this baseline does not silently adopt them.
3. Fit all R1-R8 as33ohm1%0805, single resistors, not older parallel68ohm
   combinations. Fit R49 normally: the V006E8R6 reset is PC5, not PD7.
   R69/R70=1k0805;R71/R73=10k0805;R72=47k0805;C6=100nF0805.
   Fit R11/R12=4.7k only on the one pull-up tile per whole connected I2C bus.
4. Fit64 XL-2012UOC per front face. Pad1=cathode/L-row, pad2=anode/S-column;
   follow actual package polarity marks, not reel direction or mirror intuition.
5. Inspect all fine-pitch pins and MOSFET orientation; compare the full BOM/map.
   Check unpowered VCC-GND for solder shorts. Check supply/contact polarity.
   SWIO J2/bare pads are in parallel; fit the optional header only if wanted.

Initial electrical checks after assembly (not executed by this package)
Use a current-limited external regulated3.3V supply with common ground; keep
within3.135-3.465V design range. Do not apply5V or assume programmer supplies
power. First power one tile with LEDs held blank; inspect rail and idle current
before connecting an array. R72 should pull LED_BLANK high at reset, disabling
U2 pin4/G2A and all column PMOS switches. Verify this physically before
running a display program. R73/C6 provide nominal1ms reset RC, not a measured
reset timing guarantee. Keep test logs per board.

No V006 application/custom loader is included or flashed. Do not load the
existing V003/v3.1 firmware. Preserve per-tile SWIO and reset access for later
scoped programming. Firmware must leave PA6 high-impedance (bonded with PA1
on physical pin17), use the reviewed remaps, initialize rows/addresses while
blanked and release blank only after bounded scan timing is established.
Firmware development, optical/current/waveform/network/mechanical qualification
remain later work; this plan does not claim those tests passed.

Joining tiles
Power off all tiles before soldering or changing connections. Keep every tile
upright; match numbered solder contacts and rails. Edge holes are ordinary
0.8mmPTH, not castellated/plated edges. Nominal staples span2.54mm across
abutting PCBs, using0.5mmwire. Do not force fit until real-board tolerance
and solder access are checked. J2 is0.6mmPTH; M2 holes are2.2mmNPTH.
West RX1 receives upstream east TX1; south RX2 receives upstream north TX2.
Never join two outputs. SDA/SCL/GND/3V3 are shared. Replies/recovery need
future I2C firmware; two USARTs are not four bidirectional neighbor ports.
Choose a five-tile arrangement and power injection based on measured array
current/drop; do not extrapolate to arbitrary tile counts. Earlier diffuser/
carrier models do not fit49.6mm square/6.2mmpixels. No mechanics are qualified.
