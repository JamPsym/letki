Method
Read the manufacturer's component/pin drawings visually, including top versus
bottom views. Export the current schematic netlist. Construct a separate full
expected circuit map from those pin functions and the intended matrix, rather
than merely compare with the historical study schematic. Verify every numbered
pad in the actual PCB against that map. Undo each footprint's rear-side flip
and rotation in memory and compare embedded pad positions/numbers/sizes/drills
with its local library. Check physical numbering against the component drawings.
No file is saved through pcbnew.

347 unique schematic component/pin pairs checked; 350 physical numbered pads
checked (J2 has three additional parallel bare pads). All 146 footprint pad
geometries match their local library definitions after placement normalization.
No differences. The separate fresh ERC/DRC/parity and copper-connectivity
checks from the preceding production audit also passed on these same CAD hashes.

Detailed findings
U1 CH32V003F4P6: correct TSSOP20 package and counterclockwise top-view numbering.
VDD9, VSS7, SWIO/PD1 pin18, TX/PD5 pin2, RX/PD6 pin3, SDA/PC1 pin11 and
SCL/PC2 pin12 agree with the manufacturer's F4P6 pinout. No use of the different
F4U6 QFN or A4M6 SOP pin assignment. All row/address GPIO connections checked.

U2 SN74HC138PWR: correct TSSOP16 numbering. VCC16/GND8, active-low enables4/5
at GND, active-high enable6 at VCC. Address A/B/C pins1/2/3 connect to
PC7/PC6/PC5 with their 4.7k pull-ups. Y0..Y7 pins15/14/13/12/11/10/9/7 feed
the matching PMOS gates through R15..R22. Active-low selection suits PMOS drive.

Q1..Q8 AO3415A: gate1/source2/drain3. Source goes to VCC, drain to an LED
anode column, gate to the decoder through 1k with 10k gate-to-source pull-up.
Q9..Q16 DMN2056U-7: gate1/source2/drain3. Source goes to GND, drain to the
row-current resistor, gate to its MCU output through 1k with 10k pulldown.
The physical SOT23 two-lead side has gate/source in the correct order; the
single-lead side is drain. Both body diodes oppose the intended off-state
supply-to-load current path. All sixteen devices were checked individually.

D1..D64 XL-2012UOC: manufacturer pin1=cathode, pin2=anode. Every anode belongs
to its PMOS-driven column; every cathode belongs to its row's single 33R
resistor and NMOS sink. No disconnected cathode row or resistor bypass in the
expected topology. All64 cathode-side silk closures and Fab chamfers agree
with pad1. LEDs are intentionally rotated180degrees from original v3:
with the saved board viewed from the front, cathode/pad1 is on the RIGHT.
This is the intended v3.1 drive polarity, not an accidental KiCad flip.

J2 Molex53047-0310: 1.25mm pitch and circuit numbering retained; pin1 VCC,
pin2 SWIO, pin3 GND. Each bare pad is electrically parallel with its same-number
header hole. Rear mounting mirrors its apparent position in a front view;
use pin numbers and the component-side view when making the cable. The known
0.6mm-hole/1.6mm-board mechanical adaptation is separate from pin correctness.

J1/J3 edge contacts: matching Y positions connect VCC/VCC/SDA/SCL/UART/GND/GND
across an abutted seam. Right TX feeds the next tile's left RX; supply and I2C
are not crossed. Both 1k UART series resistors and the RX-side pull-up match
the intended topology. Only one tile should populate R11/R12 I2C pulls.

All capacitor endpoints connect across VCC/GND; the selected capacitors are
nonpolar ceramic parts. All passive endpoints, gate/drain biases, decoder
address pulls and intentionally disconnected MCU pins are included in the
complete expected map. Driver review agrees with physical pixel placement:
bit0 is the top row through PD7/Q16; decoder address0 selects the left column.
The earlier R39 pull-up and D9..D16 cathode disconnections remain repaired.
