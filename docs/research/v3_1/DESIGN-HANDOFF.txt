ELECTRICAL QUESTIONS THAT DETERMINE THE CIRCUIT

1. Limit the SUM of eight LED currents through the selected HC138 output.
   TI specifies VOL at 4mA with VCC=4.5V (0.33V maximum across the SN74
   operating-temperature range). Its 25mA per-output / 50mA supply-ground
   absolute limits are stress ratings, not operating targets. PWM average
   current does not justify exceeding instantaneous output limits.
   The decoder datasheet has no equivalent loaded 3.3V test row; do not
   interpolate one into a guaranteed specification.

2. Compare two passive architectures while retaining matrix polarity/pins:
   A. Per-row resistance: more consistent pixel current. Example stock-only
      series combination 10k 1% + 4.7k 5% on each row gives minimum initial
      resistance 14365 ohm. At an assumed 5.25V maximum supply, even zero LED
      and driver drops bound a fully lit column to 8*5.25/14365 = 2.924mA.
      This needs 16 row resistors total (8 additional lands), with a much
      smaller normal LED current and potentially inadequate brightness.
   B. Per-column resistance: add one stock 4.7k 5% between each decoder output
      and cathode bus; retain stock row resistors. Minimum initial 4465 ohm
      bounds total selected-column current to 5.25/4465 = 1.176mA, ignoring
      other drops. Current per pixel falls as more rows light. Assess that
      visible pattern dependence before choosing this smaller resistor set.
   These are preliminary upper bounds under an ASSUMED supply ceiling, not
   selected populations or guaranteed optical outputs. Add TCR/drift and
   resistor ratings; check inactive-column levels, leakage and LED reverse
   voltage. Use identified extra resistor values to improve the tradeoff.

3. Choose external common-rail voltage and tolerance before claiming logic
   margins. WCH v1.3 permits 2.7-5.5V without ADC; its output table separates
   TTL and CMOS port cases, with CMOS VOH only 2.3V in the eight-pin 8mA test.
   HC138 VIH is 3.15V at VCC=4.5V. Establish applicable port/load guarantees
   for PC5/PC6/PC7; a light-load expectation or working v3 alone is insufficient.
   3.3V reduces LED headroom and has sparse HC138 loaded-output data; 5V
   increases interface-level requirements. Neither supply is selected yet.

4. Direct UART and I2C track the chosen common rail. Do not assume the
   existing 3.3V adapter is valid at 5V or claim hot-plug/unpowered tolerance.
   Stock resistors may limit/damp current but do not recreate v4's buffered
   interfaces or a general level shifter. SWIO reference/power must be reviewed.

5. Decide acceptable brightness with low selected-column current. At the
   existing 100us blank per 500us column slot, each pixel's maximum duty is
   about 10%, before gamma dimming. XL-2012UOC's 20mA forward-voltage data
   does not guarantee its behavior at sub-mA currents. Ask the testing session
   for current/voltage/optical evidence after an independently authorized test.

6. Size a complete chain from measured load, voltage drop, bus capacitance
   and adapter timing. Physical two-board I2C/UART remain pending in the copied
   records. No board, serial port or programmer was accessed by this session.
