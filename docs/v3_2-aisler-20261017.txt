Provider profile differences
The selected AISLER stack is 35/35/35/35 um, whereas the milestone's authored
stack is 35/17.5/17.5/35 um. ENIG also differs from its HAL SnPb finish.
The thicker inner planes are not by themselves a rejection, but prior numerical
power-plane estimates use the old stack. Keep 35 um outer copper for this
routing: AISLER's 70 um option requires 0.175 mm trace/space, exceeding existing
0.150 mm tracks.

Three verified items requiring an AISLER-specific revision or supplier agreement
1. All299 vias are tented in the milestone. AISLER does not support tented vias.
   Open-via manufacturing would change the intended solder-mask coverage.
2. Copper at all28 rear edge-connection pads and one zone object on each of
   F.Cu/In1.Cu/In2.Cu falls below AISLER's 0.300 mm edge clearance. The measured
   overall minimum is 0.250 mm.
3. One pair of via drill edges is separated by 0.265685 mm; AISLER requires
   0.300 mm. UUIDs: 4acbe5c5-9166-458b-94f8-cfa5311afad5 and
   b326e92f-e002-4140-8783-7b3ab9020f61. Next closest pair was 0.469029 mm.
