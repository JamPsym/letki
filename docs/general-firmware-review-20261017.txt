   overlap, especially preserve-current vs capacity replacement and frame formats.
2. Fast flash register sequence, address aliases, linker load end/bounds, buffer
   alignment, timed controller waits, readback verification, manifest interruption
   semantics and recovery. No option/protection/mass-erase exposure. Inspect
   stopped blanking and interrupt management; execution/write behavior still
   requires physical qualification against stored WCH vendor references.
3. UART cutover/forwarding and RX service margin; duplicate/stale IDs, per-node
   sequence wrap, packet errors, queue ownership, partial commit and reconnect.
4. I2C general-call beacon and error/reset behavior. RM v1.9 documents ENGC and
   GENCALL; broadcast is used only for time. A +1 ms nominal wire compensation is
   an estimate. Clock regressions latch until a new UART session. Verify actual
   loaded-playback drift/skew, bus errors, head/receiver restarts and recovery.
5. Free-running SysTick STRE=0/STCLK=HCLK/STIE scheduling; compare rollover,
   missed interrupts and wall-time continuity through flash maintenance. Scan is
   nominal2 kHz/250 Hz with unchanged PWM/blanking driver, physically unmeasured.
6. PC deadline/clock mapping under chain asymmetry and OS/audio jitter. COMMIT
   is staging readiness; STATUS PRESENTED is a local scan latch, not optical readback.
   A broken link during commit can leave a partial frame; no global rollback claim.
7. Bauds: one-second prepare/drain, two-second confirm timeout, ten-second idle
   lease, reset115200, and return-to115200 on normal Driver.close. Profile CPU and
   UART queue margin before recommending faster rates. No hardware rates changed.
8. Migration requires all five matching images. Existing text/PWM receivers cannot
   forward this binary protocol correctly. Preserve all working full readbacks,
   identify UUIDs and roles immediately before each write, verify programmed images,
   preserve option bytes/programmer power and close serial readers/probe terminals.
