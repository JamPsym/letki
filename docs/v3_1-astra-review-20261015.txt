PRIORITIZED FINDINGS

1. P1 — R39 has lost its VCC connection, leaving decoder A0 floating on HIGH.
   Schematic: hw/ledkiv3.1/ledkiv3_1.kicad_sch:23141, R39 pin1 at
   (26.67,49.53); separate VCC label at (26.67,44.45).
   Netlist: R39.1 is alone on unconnected-(R39-Pad1). R39.2 joins U1.17
   (PC7) and U2.1 (A) on /ADDR0. The pulse driver's display_init configures
   PC5/6/7 as open-drain outputs (display.c:73-76); its HIGH command releases
   the pad. Therefore alternate column addresses have no defined high level.
   This can select the wrong column or leave the HC input at an undefined
   level. R40/R41 still correctly pull ADDR1/2 to VCC.
   Recommendation: restore R39.1-to-VCC wiring; verify that the actual XML
   net contains both endpoints, then rerun ERC and address/pin audit.

2. P1 — D9–D16 cathodes are disconnected from their row-current resistor.
   Schematic: hw/ledkiv3.1/ledkiv3_1.kicad_sch:10662, L2 label at
   (194.31,132.08); disconnected vertical wire at x194.31, y121.92..134.62.
   Netlist: /L2 contains ONLY R2.1. D9.1 through D16.1 form the separate
   Net-(D10-K) net. Thus this entire physical row lacks its intended sink
   path through R2/Q10 and cannot be driven normally. The other seven
   cathode-row nets and all eight anode-column nets are correct.
   Recommendation: join the matrix cathode bus to L2; verify the resulting
   net contains R2.1 plus all eight D9–D16 cathodes. Fix before F8 because
   synchronization could otherwise propagate this error into the routed PCB.

   Root's delta against the earlier clean schematic independently identifies
   exactly these nine changed pin-net assignments: R39.1 and D9–D16.1.
   These are saved-schematic regressions, not consequences of the documented
   pending 0603/button PCB synchronization.

ELECTRICAL CHECKS THAT PASSED / CONDITIONAL ENGINEERING ACCEPTANCE

- Actual netlist topology: Q1–Q8 are high-side AO3415A PMOS, source pin2=VCC,
  drain pin3=anode column, gate pin1 driven through1k by the correct active-low
  HC138 output, with10k gate-source pulls. Q9–Q16 are low-side DMN2056U-7,
  source2=GND, drain3=K1..K8, gate1 through1k from MCU with10k toGND.
  All16 gate networks and all8 drain/current-resistor networks have the
  intended membership. Selected SOT23 footprint pin numbering matches both
  manufacturers' G1/S2/D3 diagrams. LED pin1=K/pin2=A is consistent with
  the reversed-polarity architecture. No added IC or direct LED GPIO load.

- Proposed procurement overlay is still separate from CAD. Its5%1k gates and
  1%10k biases are electrically reasonable; E12 is the nominal-value series.
  With1k at+5% and10k at-1%, divider factor=9.9/10.95=0.9041.
  WCH's conservative CMOS VOH minimum2.3V gives NMOS VGS>=2.079V. Use
  DMN2056U's guaranteed85mohm at1.5V, not its2.5V rating, for a conservative
  lower-gate calculation. At58.26mA this is ~4.95mV drain drop at25C.
  Conditional on HC138 VOL<=0.4V, minimum PMOS |VGS| is
  (3.135-0.4)*0.9041=2.473V. Its65mohm specification at1.8V is sufficient;
  at eight58.26mA branches this gives ~30.3mV column drop at25C.
  The TI table has no loaded3.3V VOL guarantee; that condition and real
  switching remain prototype measurements, already acknowledged in the design.
  Threshold voltage alone was NOT used as an on-resistance guarantee.

- Gate resistors bound a worst initial step to3.465/950=3.65mA per pin,
  below the WCH8mA characterization load and HC13825mA absolute pin limit.
  All8 simultaneous MCU gate steps total29.2mA; static row gate-bias current
  is only~0.32mA per active pin. Adding the documented20mA core/5mA other-I/O
  allowances and three address-pull sinks keeps the illustrative VSS budget
  below80mA (VDD absolute ceiling100mA). This is an engineering budget,
  not a measured worst-case MCU core or transient current guarantee.
  0603 gate/bias power is comfortably below100mW: a hypothetical persistent
  full-rail drop on950ohm is12.64mW;10k at-1% dissipates1.21mW.

- R1–R8 are single33R1%0805/125mW parts. The3% resistance allowance is
  additional engineering margin, not their catalogue tolerance. Conditional
 58.26mA is below the LED80mA pulse ceiling at25C, but above20mA continuous.
  Each LED has nominal80us/1ms=8%duty; a shared resistor can conduct8such
  pulses per frame=64%duty. Its conditional average power is69.54mW, not
  8% of pulse power. LED Vf1.6V remains an assumption, not a guaranteed hot
  minimum. Existing physical current/tail/temperature qualification is needed.

- Pulse driver source uses GPIO-LOW blanking before address changes,
  TIM1/2 PWM2+one-pulse mode,110us windows and at most80us commanded HIGH.
  It boots brightness0 and intentionally enables100% on command0. A missed
  next SysTick need not hold the hardware pulse HIGH. Actual LED-current
  tails, recurrence under interrupt load, timer behavior on debug halt, and
  temperature derating remain unverified; this review does not upgrade those
  documented limits to a guarantee. Original v3/repetitive study images are
  incompatible. R49-open/R38-fitted first provisioning correctly isolates
  factory PD7/NRST from its10k pulldown; separately verified GPIO option
  configuration is required before fitting R49.

- R50–R57 remain10k VCC-to-NMOS-drain/cathode bias resistors. They neither
  replace the gate pulldowns nor independently protect pulse current. Their
  net placement is consistent with off-row cathode bias/ghosting control;
  optional removal needs the already-proposed physical A/B test. No removal
  recommendation or authorization is inferred here.

- U1/U2 power/ground and decoder enables are correct. C1/C3=100nF and
  C4=10uF connect across VCC/GND; removed C2/C5 were button RC parts.
  No new supply-capacitance disconnect was found. Supply is externally
  regulated3.3V±5%; the documented two-ended SAME-source five-tile feed and
  ~0.5A peak/tile budget are reasonable qualification assumptions. Schematic
  capacitance and historical copper geometry do not prove transient impedance.

- UART: U1.PD5→R66→J1.5; J3.5→R67→U1.PD6; R68 pulls the edge side of RX
  HIGH. This is coherent for the directional3.3V chain. I2C PC1/SDA and
  PC2/SCL join both edges; fit only one R11/R12 pull pair. J2 pins1/2/3 are
  VCC/PD1-SWIO/GND. PD0/PA2 are intentionally NC after button removal.
  The three ADDR nets are decoder select bits, not persistent tile addresses.
  Five-tile text-chain control is explicitly deferred, not provided by this
  standalone pulse image. Interface rise times/hot-plug remain unqualified.
