Prototype-order gate
The CAD/package specifies ordinary tented 0.6/0.3mm vias. The project records
still leave the fabricator Tented/Plugged decision and preview unconfirmed.
JLCPCB's current documentation distinguishes tenting from ink plugging and
states a 0.35mm restriction near soldermask openings for ink plugging. The
project's 0.36mm via-centre-to-SMT-pad guard is not a certification of that
separate plugging process. Do not assume selecting Plugged is equivalent to
this package's tented intent. Confirm the actual CAM treatment and preserve
solderable component/contact holes. No fabrication upload/order was performed.

Correct the current template and provide a clearly versioned assembly addendum
or new checked handoff before use. Preserve the accepted frozen snapshot.

population is 697 SMT plus five headers. Refresh the current sourcing and
cost worksheet before buying; price/stock checks were outside this audit.
