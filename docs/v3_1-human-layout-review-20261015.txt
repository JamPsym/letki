Ground-island follow-up:
Native saved polygon membership locates the U2.8 GND via at
(24.785,10.052840) in the small isolated region 0, separated from the
main region by surrounding signal clearances (/S5 and /ADDR2 nearby).
The Q12.2 GND via (33.386,23.516) lands in right-side region 2, whereas
Q13.2 via (33.136,18.766) and Q11.2 via (33.136,28.766) land in main
region 1. The DRC Q13-to-Q12 endpoint pair describes separation between
clusters; it does not mean Q13 is the isolated device. Thus the practical
repairs are U2.8 to main GND and the Q12-source region to main GND.
A via assigned GND only joins copper it physically touches; another via
inside the same island alone will not bridge the split. Use an outer-layer
GND bridge to a connected region or move the cutting signal routes.
