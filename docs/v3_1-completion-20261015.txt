Power interpretation:
A plane can carry power without duplicate rails if its connections/necks are
adequate. Here the signal cuts and narrow gateways required explicit bridges
and feeds. The main 17.5um GND sheet model gives approximately4.14mOhm between
paired left/right contacts at a0.1mm mesh, excluding vias, solder, contacts,
AC inductance, thermal behavior and the full five-tile loop. This is a model,
not a measured current/temperature rating or proof of five-tile regulation.
Use external regulated3.3V, feed BOTH ends from the SAME source, and qualify
rail drop, LED pulse/current/tails and temperature on the assembled chain.
